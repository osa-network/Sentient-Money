# Sentient-Money
Money is an illusion — and in this game, you're the one creating it.

Click Mint to conjure Sents into existence. But Sents have a will of their own. Any money sitting in your account will spend itself on Frippery — abstract consumption that accumulates in your storage. Sents that don't fit in your account spill out into the world, slowly buying up the tiles around you.

Your goal is to own all the tiles on the map. But if Frippery fills your storage and overflows, the game ends.

Controls

Mint — create Sents and push them into your account
Hold Discipline — slow the rate at which your money spends itself
Expand Account — pay to increase how much your account can hold
Boost — inject Sents directly into your account (limited uses)
Click a tile — buy it if you can afford it

Tips

Frippery also generates Bills — a recurring drain on your balance
The last tile is always the hardest. Don't let its price outrun your mint rate
There's more than one way to win
